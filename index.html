At widths below 600px: - Collapse navigation links into a menu button. - Use a single-column services layout. - Reduce dashboard panes to stacked panels. - Keep CTA buttons full width. - Reduce decorative grid opacity so the content remains readable. The most effective visual balance is a text-heavy left hero column paired with a high-density operational dashboard on the right. That combination communicates both personal expertise and enterprise-level delivery capability within the first viewport.
